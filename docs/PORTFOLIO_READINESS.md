## Shared review criteria

Authenticate with Pi and verify identity server-side. Use the existing app registration and network-specific credentials. Keep prices, product identifiers and wallet confirmations aligned. Restore paid entitlements from verified backend records. Show public privacy and terms links. Limit collected information to operational needs and described analytics.

Maintain source/build mapping and real-device acceptance evidence. A ready deployment indicates a successful build; it does not establish every authenticated action or Pi approval.

## App-specific alignment

IIOU equal splitting excludes the creator's own share, distributes the entered amount in seven-decimal Pi units and allows review before creation. Shared links require participant authorization. Repayment records and optional support payments serve separate purposes.

Sticker.pi starts a new player with a free pack. The onboarding action uses the existing recoverable pack-opening flow. Album and pack ownership are backend-authoritative. Peer-to-peer trading and Mainnet A2U gameplay payouts remain subject to their actual availability.

LaunchNest verifies website control and separately moderates listings. Declared listing status should stay identifiable as developer-supplied unless independently verified. A catalog necessarily links to other app destinations; its directory use case must be accurately explained during Pi review.

Idle Realm uses Cloudflare Pages/D1 for its Mainnet architecture. Council Patronage is a per-era optional 0.1 Pi purchase, and English/Simplified Chinese is selected locally by the player.
