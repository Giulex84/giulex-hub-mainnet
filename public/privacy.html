Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Arena is a skill-based memory game built for Pi Network. This policy explains the limited information Arena processes to provide sign-in, persistent gameplay, Daily Arena, asynchronous PvP, leaderboards and optional purchases.

Information processed

When you authorize Pi sign-in, Arena receives a Pi access token in the app and sends that token to Arena's backend. The backend validates it with Pi Network's /v2/me endpoint. Arena then uses the app-specific Pi UID and, if authorized, your Pi username. The client-provided user object is not used as the server source of truth.

Payments and purchases

If you purchase Premium or a Daily Replay Ticket, Pi Network processes the blockchain payment. Arena's backend receives the Pi payment identifier and transaction identifier needed to approve, complete, verify, and recover that payment. Arena stores only the minimum entitlement, ticket balance and fulfillment records needed to deliver the purchase and prevent duplicate fulfillment. Arena does not request or store your wallet passphrase or private keys.

Gameplay data

Arena stores Classic progress, Daily attempts and best results, temporary PvP matches, replay-ticket balances and leaderboard entries under your verified app-specific Pi UID. Leaderboards display your authorized Pi username with moves and gameplay points. Daily and PvP records are normally temporary; purchase and Premium records may be retained longer for fulfillment and dispute handling.

Service providers and technical data

Arena uses Pi Network for authentication and Pi payments, Vercel for application hosting, and a server-side Redis-compatible key-value database to persist purchases and gameplay records. These providers may process ordinary technical data such as IP address, device/browser information, request time and diagnostic logs to deliver and secure the service. Server credentials are not exposed to the browser.

Retention and your choices

Temporary Daily and PvP data normally expires automatically. Premium entitlement, associated payment fulfillment records and gameplay progress may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service, restore purchased features and progress, prevent duplicate fulfillment, resolve payment issues, and comply with applicable obligations. You may contact Arena to request access, correction or deletion of personal gameplay/profile data where applicable. Some payment and anti-duplication records may need to be retained. Deleting the app or local browser data does not cancel or reverse a blockchain payment.
